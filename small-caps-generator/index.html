Turn normal text into ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘꜱ using look-alike Unicode characters — for bios, usernames, or anywhere plain text works. Converts as you type, nothing is sent to a server.

Letters are swapped for small-capital Unicode look-alikes (from the Latin Extended and IPA Extensions blocks). A few letters (q, s, x) fall back to the closest available character or stay as-is. Numbers, spaces, and symbols are never changed. Rendering depends on the font wherever you paste it — most modern apps and social platforms display these fine.
